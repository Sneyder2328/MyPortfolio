Privacy policy – Notepad

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android app “Notepad” (the “App”) handles, why, and what choices you have. The App is developed and published by Sneyder Angulo (Twismart), who is the data controller. Contact: info.twismart@gmail.com.

In short
  • You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your notes, lists and settings stay on your device. We never see them.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob on the note list (never in the editor). Google may collect device data, such as the advertising ID, to serve and measure those ads.
  • We don’t sell your data.

1. Your notes, stored on your device

The App stores in your device’s internal storage:

  • your notes and checklists: title, text or list items, colour, whether they are pinned, and when they were created and last edited;
  • notes you moved to the trash, which are deleted automatically after 30 days or when you empty the trash;
  • your preferences (theme, text size, grid or list layout, sort order).

The content of your notes is never sent to us, to Google or to any other third party, and the ads never use it. This data is deleted when you delete the notes, uninstall the App or clear its data in the device settings. If system backup is turned on, the device may include it in your backup.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

The App shows a banner on the note list through Google AdMob, a Google service (Google Ireland Limited in the European Economic Area and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). To serve ads, limit how often you see each one, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and system information (model, operating system version, language);
  • your interactions with ads (impressions and clicks) and technical diagnostics about ad loading.

If you allow it (or where the law does not require consent), Google may also use this data to show you personalized ads. Google may process this data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its policy. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not receive data from Google that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown per country.

3. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows you a Google consent form before requesting ads. You can accept, decline or choose the purposes your data is used for. If you decline, you can keep using the App normally; only non-personalized or limited ads are shown.

You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. This option appears when the consent form applies to you.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for storing or accessing information on your device and for personalized ads. For purposes Google processes under legitimate interest, such as security and fraud prevention, you can object in the same form.

4. Controlling personalized ads

Wherever you are, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off interest-based ads in your device settings (on Amazon Fire tablets, usually Settings → Privacy → Advertising ID; on other Android devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also review your ad preferences in Google’s My Ad Center. If you live in a U.S. state with a privacy law (such as California), this is also how you opt out of ads based on your activity in this App.

5. Features that use your device’s services

  • Share: when you share a note, its text is handed to the app you pick in Android’s share menu, which handles it under its own policy.
  • Read aloud: the App passes the note’s text to your device’s text-to-speech engine (for example, the one built into Fire tablets or Google’s). The engine is provided by your device and works under its provider’s policy.
  • Backups and .txt export: notes are written only to the file and place you choose in the system file picker, and imported only from a file you pick. The App has no access to your other files.

6. Permissions

The App uses Internet and network-state access only to load ads and the consent form, and the advertising ID permission for those ads. It does not request your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or access to your storage.

7. Retention and security

We run no servers for this App and keep no data about you. Your notes stay on your device until you delete them. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policy. The AdMob SDK’s communications are encrypted.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the U.S.), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we keep no data that identifies you, the most direct way is to delete your notes or clear the App’s data, change your choice in Privacy choices, and use the advertising controls above. You can also email us at info.twismart@gmail.com or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also mention it on the App’s store listing.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, email info.twismart@gmail.com.
