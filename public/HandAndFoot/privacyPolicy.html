Privacy policy – Hand and Foot

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game “Hand and Foot” (the “App”) handles, why, and what choices you have. The App is developed and published by Sneyder Angulo (Twismart), who is the data controller. Contact: info.twismart@gmail.com.

In short
  • You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game in progress and your settings stay on your device. The other players are played by the computer on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data, such as the advertising ID, to serve and measure those ads.
  • We don’t sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

The App stores in your device’s internal storage:

  • the game in progress, so you can continue it later;
  • your preferences (quick or classic rules, and the speed of the other players).

This data is not sent to us or to third parties. It is deleted when a game ends, when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the device settings. If system backup is turned on, the device may include it in your backup.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (a banner at the bottom of the screen and, at most once every few minutes, a full-screen ad between rounds), a Google service (Google Ireland Limited in the European Economic Area and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). To serve ads, limit how often you see each one, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and system information (model, operating system version, language);
  • your interactions with ads (impressions and clicks) and technical diagnostics about ad loading.

If you allow it (or where the law does not require consent), Google may also use this data to show you personalized ads. Google may process this data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its policy. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not receive data from Google that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown per country.

3. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows you a Google consent form before requesting ads. You can accept, decline or choose the purposes your data is used for. If you decline, you can keep using the App normally; only non-personalized or limited ads are shown.

You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button on the App’s home screen. This option appears when the consent form applies to you.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for storing or accessing information on your device and for personalized ads. For purposes Google processes under legitimate interest, such as security and fraud prevention, you can object in the same form.

4. Controlling personalized ads

Wherever you are, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off interest-based ads in your device settings (on Amazon Fire tablets, usually Settings → Privacy → Advertising ID; on other Android devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also review your ad preferences in Google’s My Ad Center. If you live in a U.S. state with a privacy law (such as California), this is also how you opt out of ads based on your activity in this App.

5. No online play

There are no accounts, chat, leaderboards or online opponents. Cards are shuffled at random on your device. There are no purchases, coins or real-money gambling.

6. Permissions

The App uses Internet and network-state access only to load ads and the consent form. It does not request your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

7. Retention and security

We run no servers for this App and keep no data about you. Local data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policy. The AdMob SDK’s communications are encrypted.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the U.S.), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we keep no data that identifies you, the most direct way is to clear the App’s data, change your choice in Privacy choices, and use the advertising controls above. You can also email us at info.twismart@gmail.com or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also mention it on the App’s store listing.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, email info.twismart@gmail.com.
